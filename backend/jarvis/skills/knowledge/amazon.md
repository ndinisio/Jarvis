sites: amazon
- Search: https://www.amazon.co.uk/s?k=your+words (amazon.com in the US).
- Results start with "Sponsored" items; unless asked, pick the first result that is actually what was asked for.
- A product page's link contains /dp/. "Add to Basket" (UK) or "Add to Cart" (US) adds it; a dialog "Added to Basket" confirms it.
- Some products need a size, colour or quantity chosen first — a message appears if one's missing.
- Never press "Buy Now" or "Proceed to checkout" unless the user asked to buy: it's ordering, and it will be confirmed with them.
